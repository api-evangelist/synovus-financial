# Synovus Financial

> Synovus Financial Corp. was a Columbus, Georgia based regional bank holding company whose bank subsidiary, Synovus Bank, offers commercial and consumer banking, treasury and payment solutions (Synovus Gateway, the Accelerate AR/FX/Pay/Trade suite), wealth and capital markets services across Georgia, Alabama, Florida, South Carolina and Tennessee. Synovus combined with Pinnacle Financial Partners in 2026 ("Synovus and Pinnacle Financial Partners are now one firm" — synovus.com homepage); the surviving company is Pinnacle Financial Partners (pnfp.com) and investors.synovus.com now redirects to investors.pnfp.com.

## Public API surface

Synovus publishes no public API contract. What was found on 2026-09-17:

- developer.synovus.com resolves (explicit Akamai edgekey CNAME, no wildcard DNS) but answers HTTP 403 "Access Denied" on every path — the root, /docs, /openapi.json, /robots.txt, /llms.txt and every /.well-known/* document — from two separate networks. A developer host exists, and it is closed to anonymous readers.
- api.synovus.com does not resolve in DNS.
- www.synovus.com serves no /.well-known/* documents, no security.txt and no llms.txt (all 404).
- No first-party GitHub organization, Postman workspace, or package registry entry was found (github.com/Synovus is an unrelated personal account).
- Treasury and payment integrations are delivered through partner platforms rather than a documented bank API: Gateway ERP (launched October 2025) is powered by Koxa's Treasury Gateway (koxa.io); Accelerate Pay advertises ERP integration without a public reference. Maast, the embedded-finance subsidiary launched in 2022, has been wound down — maast.com now redirects to the Synovus homepage.
